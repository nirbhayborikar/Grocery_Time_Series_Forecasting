## Resume description, once implemented

Developed RetailCast, an end-to-end neural network time-series forecasting and inventory optimization system for multi-store grocery demand. Built a complete ML pipeline with temporal feature engineering, lag and rolling-window features, regional store clustering, and PyTorch neural network training, model-weight optimization, and hyperparameter tuning. Benchmarked neural forecasts against LightGBM and statistical baselines using RMSLE, MAE, and WAPE, then converted store-level predictions into cluster-level inventory recommendations to minimize excess stock, stockouts, and product waste.
